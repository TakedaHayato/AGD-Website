The Rise of Aegis Global Defense

Before Aegis Global Defense ever stood on its own, it was allied with the Aegis Global Task Force. Together, they operated as a powerful force—but alliances don’t always last. Tensions grew, disagreements escalated, and eventually, a war broke out between the two sides. What was once unity turned into conflict, shaping the future of AGD forever.

In the streets of Emergency Hamburg, the goal was clear: fight against gangs and restore peace. Amid chaos and rising violence, three founders—Hayato, Nemesis, and Tim—came together on July 14th, 2023. They weren’t just reacting to the situation—they were building something new.

They called it Aegis Global Defense (AGD).

When the group later expanded into War Tycoon, their vision became even clearer: They didn’t just want to win. They wanted to create something bigger: a force dedicated to order, strength, and above all, peace.
