The Rise of Aegis Global Defense

Aegis Global Defense was born from the chaos of Emergency Hamburg. Founded on July 14th, 2023, by Hayato, Nemesis, and Tim.

By 2026, AGD expanded its reach into War Thunder with the establishment of Sechenov's Legion (SCNV). Despite facing challenges and the loss of our co-founder TimTheKeks in 2026, the community stands stronger than ever, united by tactics and teamwork.
